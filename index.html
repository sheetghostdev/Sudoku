How to play

Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 patch holds the numbers 1 to 9 once each. Tap a square, then tap a number. Turn on Notes to pencil in small maybes.

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day, and a new one arrives at midnight. Monday and Tuesday are gentle, midweek and Sunday are classic, Friday and Saturday are tricky. Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone.
